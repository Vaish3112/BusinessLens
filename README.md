# BusinessLens

### *Sales & Business Performance Analytics*


## Overview

BusinessLens is an end-to-end analysis of sales transactions, built as the capstone project for the BeeSkilled Internship. Using data from September 2013 to December 2014 (2013 is a partial year), it shows where revenue actually turns into profit, and where it doesn't.

To do this, the analysis looks at:

- Sales and profit performance over time
- Product contribution and profitability
- Customer segment and country-level performance
- Discounting and its effect on profit margins

It closes with business insights and recommendations.

## Key Metrics

| Total Sales | Total Profit | Units Sold | Profit Margin |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 118.73M | 16.89M | 1.13M | 14.23% |

## Key Findings

- 118.73M total sales generated 16.89M profit with a 14.23% overall margin.
- Enterprise generated 19.61M in sales but −0.61M profit, making it the only loss-making segment.
- Government generated 11.39M profit and was the top profit segment across all five countries.
- Paseo led sales at 33.01M and profit at 4.80M.
- Amarilla achieved the highest product margin at 15.86%.
- Velo had the lowest product margin at 12.64%.
- High-discount transactions generated 37.37M sales at a 9.07% margin, compared with 34.63M sales and a 17.87% margin for the Low-discount band.

## Workflow

1. **Python (Pandas, Jupyter):** data cleaning, validation and exploratory analysis
2. **Excel:** pivot-based business analysis
3. **Power BI:** interactive three-page dashboard (Overview, Product Analysis, Business Analysis)
4. **Report:** findings, charts and recommendations
